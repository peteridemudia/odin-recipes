Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a layered pasta dish with meat sauce, cheese, and noodles, baked until the top is browned.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella

Steps

  1. Cook the noodles.
  2. Brown the beef and mix it with the sauce.
  3. Layer noodles, sauce, and cheese in a pan.
  4. Bake until hot and browned on top.
