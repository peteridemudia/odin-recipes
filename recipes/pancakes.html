Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are flat cakes cooked on a griddle and usually served with butter and syrup.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Butter
  • Baking powder

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients.
  2. Add the milk and eggs and stir.
  3. Pour batter onto a hot griddle.
  4. Flip when bubbles form and cook the other side.
